# TraceSpace Product Guides

Product guides capture durable product intent. The roadmap tracks delivery
status. Guides define what each capability should mean for users, what source
material it expects, what review language is allowed, what outputs are valid,
and how future validation should prove the capability.

## Guide Rule

Create or update a guide when a capability has its own:

- Target users.
- Source and data expectations.
- Review language.
- Safety boundaries.
- Output contracts.
- Validation intent.

Keep ticket-level sequencing, test checklists, and implementation status in
`ROADMAP.md`.
